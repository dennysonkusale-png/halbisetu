HalbiSetu

हल्बी Halbi · Hindi · Marathi · Sanskrit · English — community-editable
How to use. Type or speak a word to find it. If a meaning is wrong or missing, press Edit and fix it — a native speaker's correction is the most valuable data here. Your corrections are saved in this browser and can be Exported or printed with Download PDF. Entries marked OCR come from a scanned book and especially need checking; demo entries are seed data. Marathi/Sanskrit audio falls back to the Hindi voice on devices that lack those voices.
